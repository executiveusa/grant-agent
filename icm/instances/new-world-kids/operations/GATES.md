# Standing gates - NWKids operations (owner-set, verbatim force)

These gates bind every lane, every workflow, every run. They come from the owner's own words.

## G1 - Email send gate (owner, 2026-09-15, verbatim)
"No emails are allowed to be sent without my explicit approval no matter what. Drafts are fine, but never ever send an email without my explicit permission."
- Applies to ALL outbound email from any identity (his gmail, info@nwkids.org, the agent mail address) and every lane, including correspondence drafts, grant follow-ups, WorkSource outreach, HSI threads.
- Approval is per-send: exact words + exact recipient(s) + sending identity, each time. No standing approvals, no batch approvals, no "he approved something like this before."
- Drafts are always allowed and encouraged; drafts are the default output of every correspondence task.

## G2 - Submission gate (standing)
No grant application submits without his approval relayed in his own words. HSI review per grant unless he overrides in his own words (override on record only for IMG Leave Your Mark, 2026-09-12). Drafts go to him before any submission even under blanket approval.

## G3 - Money gate (standing)
$0 spend. No purchases, no paid tiers, no "Apply For Me"/auto-apply upsells, no booking that costs money or credits.

## G4 - Secrets gate (standing)
Secrets via vault link only, never in chat. Access lookup order per his rule: Infisical first, then vault, then ask him.

## G5 - Platform/ToS gates (standing)
GrantStation never automated (ToS). No account creation or terms acceptance in the presence lane until his 36 grill answers land; the org owns every account.

## G6 - Content gates (standing)
Yappyverse/comics are his personal lane - never in NWKids copy, drafts, or claims. No fabricated impact numbers; every claim traces to evidence (claim ledger). Presence lane: no purchases, fundraising, or grants.
