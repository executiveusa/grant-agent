## Seattle-new-nonprofit reality check
- P2P at the center is the RIGHT call for this exact org: no list, no budget, no events - the community connectors carry it. Seattle giving is strong but saturated; warm intros beat cold everything.
- Events/galas: OUT (negative expected value at this size, per sources 13/19).
- Corporate: real but slow; Phase 3, never the engine.
- DAF/matching: compounding layers that only pay once a donor base exists - build the data plumbing now, harvest later.
- Monthly giving: the survival metric; converts the P2P wave into predictable revenue.
