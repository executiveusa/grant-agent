Play: LOIs die in the first lines. Lead with the PROBLEM, not the org: one or two short human sentences on the issue and who is affected. Then the credibility bridge - briefly how you address it and why you are positioned (a summary, not a program menu). Warning from source: an LOI is not a donor appeal - heavy emotion and urgency language backfire with funders.
