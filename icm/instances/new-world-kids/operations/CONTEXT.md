# NWKids Operations — Routing

Live operations workspace for the assigned NWKids grant agent (Instinct). The instance folder above carries the canonical org facts and product context; this folder carries HOW the recurring operational work runs, one folder per lane, per ICM convention.

## Lanes
- `01_state-compliance/` — WA SOS standing, annual reports, registered agent, corporate records. Cadence: monthly check + 60-day alerts before 05/31/2027 expiration.
- `02_grant-pipeline/` — the live opportunity set (Skip, IMG, Galaxy, NMF Small Sparks, FundRazr) with statuses, deadlines, gates.
- `03_presence-operator/` — Nonprofit Presence Launch workflow. PARKED pending the owner's 36 grill answers; org profile skeleton lives here.
- `04_hsi-correspondence/` — Humanitarian Social Innovations (fiscal sponsor) communications. DRAFT-ONLY; nothing sends without owner review.
- `05_accounts-verification/` — org account inventory and verification handling. Org owns every account; credentials via Infisical/vault first, never chat.
- `06_learning-log/` — dated lessons that change how the lanes run; workflows update from here.

## Standing rules (owner-set, apply to every lane)
1. No external submission/sending without the owner's explicit approval, relayed per item. Drafts always precede submission, even under blanket approval.
2. HSI review gate applies to grant/fundraising copy unless the owner overrides per grant in his own words (override on record: IMG Leave Your Mark, 2026-09-12).
3. No invented nonprofit facts. Unknowns become "Needs you" items, never filled prose.
4. $0 spend. Dismiss all upsells. No purchases or financial commitments.
5. Yappyverse/comics is the owner's personal lane - never in NWKids fundraising copy.
6. GrantStation is never automated (ToS).
7. Live site content (nwkids.org) outranks repo copy where they diverge; flag divergences, don't silently merge.
