# New World Kids — Grant Agent Context

## Role in Prototype V1
New World Kids is customer zero. This workspace exists to prove the funding workflow on a real organization before any broad market claims are made.

## Mission frame
Teach practical life skills through real projects organized around four pillars:
- Food
- Water
- Energy
- Shelter

## Current program frame
- Culture Shock — Seattle, Washington.
- Proyecto Indigo Azul — Puerto Vallarta / rural Mexico.

## Public legal/trust facts allowed in this prototype
- Legal corporation name: THE NORTH WEST KIDS.
- Washington nonprofit corporation; effective May 16, 2022; UBI 604 917 764.
- Humanitarian Social Innovations is the fiscal sponsor under the signed sponsorship agreement available to the organization.

## Funding governance
- Do not attribute Humanitarian Social Innovations' federal nonprofit status or EIN directly to New World Kids.
- Grant/fundraising copy and submissions that fall under the fiscal sponsorship agreement must follow the sponsor's review/approval requirements.
- No worker may invent or silently normalize missing facts.
- No external submission is enabled in Prototype V1.

## Evidence rule
A factual claim may be used in matching or a draft only when it has a traceable source in the organization's canonical ICM/evidence set or the official opportunity source. Unknown facts become `Needs you` work.

## Prototype funding priority
Start with U.S./Washington opportunities connected to youth development, education, practical life skills, food systems, community resilience, and program capacity. International/private-foundation lanes are future adapters and must not be implied to be complete in V1.

## Product philosophy
**Software that gets you out of your seat.**

The system should remove repetitive research, triage, coordination, drafting preparation, and deadline work. Humans stay responsible for mission judgment, relationships, factual corrections, consequential approvals, and any external submission until a later release explicitly proves a safe policy-controlled path.
