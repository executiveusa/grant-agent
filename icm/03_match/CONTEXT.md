# 03 Match Contract

## Reads
Candidate packet + canonical organization/program context + official opportunity materials.

## Does
Apply hard disqualifiers first. Then analyze mission/program/geography/population/funding-use fit, amount, timing, relationship requirements, likely effort, evidence gaps, and strategic value. Separate confirmed facts from inference.

## Writes
Ranked shortlist: `strong`, `possible`, `weak`, `ineligible`, each with reasons, citations, confidence, missing facts, estimated effort, and a recommended action.

## Human check
Grant Lead selects opportunities to pursue when capacity or strategy tradeoffs matter.

## Exit
No opportunity reaches writing without official-source eligibility review.
