# 01 Onboarding Contract

## Reads
- organization-provided website/documents
- approved connected sources
- `_shared/product-thesis.md`
- `_shared/security-privacy.md`

## Does
Create the organization's canonical ICM: identity, legal/fiscal status, programs, geographies, populations served, goals, budgets, evidence, outcomes, prior applications, funder history, team roles, connected systems, source provenance, and approval policy. Ask only for facts that cannot be resolved from authorized sources.

## Writes
`workspaces/<tenant>/organization/` plus onboarding gap report and source inventory.

## Human check
Owner/Grant Lead confirms high-risk identity/legal/financial facts, team roles, and action permissions.

## Exit
Organization brain passes the walk test and has an explicit list of unknown/missing evidence.
