# Prototype V1 — Learning / Proof Ledger

Prototype V1 exists to create evidence, not marketing claims.

For each real New World Kids run, capture:
- funding need and timestamp
- exact dependency revisions
- candidate count
- candidates rejected before writing
- rejection reasons
- missing facts escalated to humans
- factual corrections made by humans
- human active minutes
- screen minutes
- tool/runtime cost where measurable
- final pursuit decision
- official source URLs
- later application/report outcome

## Watchdog questions
- What did the human have to repeat?
- What source lane was missing?
- What candidate should have been rejected earlier?
- What factual correction recurred?
- What permission did a worker not need?
- What interruption could have been avoided?
- Did the system actually reduce time in the chair?

Do not convert a single successful run into a generalized performance claim. Repeated measured evidence is required before publishing savings, throughput, award-rate, or quality numbers.
