# 07 Learning Contract

## Reads
Research outcomes, human edits, critic reports, portal traces, submissions, awards/declines, support questions, and Watchdog friction observations.

## Does
Identify reusable facts, better routing, missing skills, stale context, source quality changes, repeated edits, cost/latency bottlenecks, and evaluation failures. Propose updates to factory/reference files separately from organization facts.

## Writes
Learning report, proposed skill/context changes, source reliability updates, benchmark cases, and cost/performance metrics.

## Human check
Canonical facts, policies, and global skills are never silently rewritten from one run.

## Exit
Approved improvements are versioned and the relevant benchmark is rerun.
