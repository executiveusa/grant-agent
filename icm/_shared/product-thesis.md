# Product Thesis

## Category
Grant Agent is not an AI grant writer. It is the first vertical of a **Nonprofit Operating System**: an invisible funding department that already knows the organization.

The long-term operating philosophy is **software that gets you out of your seat**. AI should remove screen-bound administrative work, not merely accelerate a human into doing more of it.

## Customer job
When a nonprofit needs to fund a program, the team needs to know which opportunities are worth pursuing and turn organizational context into complete, compliant work without re-explaining the organization, hunting for files, living in databases, or retyping the same answers.

## Current barrier
The work is fragmented across grant databases, browsers, portals, email, Drive/Dropbox, spreadsheets, old applications, budgets, staff memory, calendars, funder websites, finance, board approvals, and reporting obligations. Writing is only one step.

AI often accelerates individual tasks while leaving the human in the chair coordinating every handoff. Grant Agent is designed to remove the coordination burden as well as the typing burden.

## Distinctive value
Grant Agent compounds organizational context, uses deterministic/source-cited discovery before model judgment, coordinates specialist agents under one orchestrator, works multiplayer with the human team, can operate authorized tools and grant portals, preserves portable institutional memory in ICM, and supports a local/private execution path.

QM provides the multiplayer identity/scope/policy/audit/sandbox fabric. Hermes provides the Grant Director cognition/orchestration layer. ICM remains the canonical business brain. Specialist workers are ephemeral and bounded.

## Positioning statement
For nonprofit teams without enough operational capacity, Grant Agent is the funding operating system that already knows the organization and turns funding needs into source-cited opportunities and prepared work. Unlike grant databases or AI writing tools, it can coordinate the full lifecycle, work with the whole team, operate connected tools under explicit permissions, preserve portable institutional memory, and bring humans in primarily for judgment rather than clerical execution.

## Product promise
**Tell it what needs funding. It handles the grant work and brings people in for judgment.**

## Brand philosophy
**Software that gets you out of your seat.**

The interface should become less important as the system becomes more capable. The human states the outcome, receives useful work, answers bounded escalations, and approves consequential actions. Web chat is the first delivery surface; Slack, email, voice, mobile, ambient computing, glasses, and other future interfaces should be able to reach the same durable operating state.

## What we refuse
- chat-with-your-files dressed up as a grants department
- generic AI prose without source grounding
- opaque fit scores with no evidence
- autonomous submission without an explicit permission policy
- dashboards as the primary experience
- per-user knowledge silos
- hidden vendor lock-in
- extracting paid databases in violation of their terms
- pretending that more AI usage automatically means less human labor
- forcing customers to learn agent infrastructure to receive an outcome

## First customer
New World Kids is the proving organization. Product claims must come after real internal use.

The first objective is not SaaS revenue. It is creating a durable strategic advantage for our own nonprofit: better funding discovery, less abandoned opportunity, faster preparation, stronger evidence discipline, fewer missed deadlines, and more institutional memory.

## Long-term expansion
Funding is the wedge. The same architecture can later power fundraising, donor stewardship, reporting/compliance, volunteer operations, program administration, communications, governance, and capital readiness.

A future capital-readiness lane may help the organization become more financeable and eventually participate in mission lending through legally appropriate structures or partners. That is a regulated/high-governance expansion and is not part of the initial Grant Agent promise.

## Measured outcomes
Do not claim savings until measured. Track:
- time from funding need -> qualified shortlist
- researcher hours per qualified opportunity
- writer/reviewer hours per application
- percent of candidate opportunities rejected before writing
- deadline misses
- application completeness/error rate
- submission throughput
- award rate and award dollars, with attribution caveats
- report/renewal on-time rate
- team questions answered without blocking on one person
- human screen time required per completed funding workflow
- percentage of work completed proactively without expanding the approved risk tier
- context re-entry avoided because approved organizational knowledge already exists
