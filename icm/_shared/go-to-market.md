# Go-to-Market

## Sell capacity removed from the chair, not "AI"
The market already contains inexpensive AI grant writing and established grant databases. Grant Agent should sell **grant operations actually removed from the team's plate**.

Core message: **A grants department that already knows your organization.**

Brand philosophy: **Software that gets you out of your seat.**

Support: It finds opportunities, explains fit, prepares applications from verified context, coordinates the team, handles repetitive portal work, tracks obligations, and learns from every cycle while bringing humans in for judgment and material approvals.

## Internal-first strategy
New World Kids is customer zero and the initial reason to build the system. We should not optimize for SaaS revenue before the system creates measurable advantage internally.

Internal objectives:
- increase the number and quality of relevant funding opportunities evaluated
- reduce time wasted on poor-fit grants
- reduce repeated organizational data entry
- build a reusable evidence/document room
- increase complete applications prepared per month without lowering quality
- prevent deadline/reporting misses
- create durable institutional memory when people change
- reduce human screen time per completed grant workflow
- improve financial/capital readiness over time

Market claims follow proof, not the reverse.

## Initial external wedge
After internal proof, start with nonprofit teams that:
- pursue several grants per year
- lack dedicated full-time grant operations capacity
- have organizational knowledge scattered across people/files/apps
- lose time researching poor-fit opportunities
- repeatedly re-enter the same organizational information
- depend on one person to remember where everything is
- can supply real program/evidence data for a pilot

## Competitive frame
- Grant databases primarily improve search/access.
- Grant workflow products improve tracking and collaboration.
- AI writing products improve drafting speed.

Grant Agent must win a different category: **execution + multiplayer organizational context + portable ICM + constrained computer use + proactive work + local/private option**.

The product should be judged by work removed and outcomes prepared, not prompts sent or documents generated.

## Packaging hypotheses - validate before publishing
### Internal / Founding deployment
New World Kids and selected founding nonprofits. Instrument every workflow. No inflated ROI claims.

### Core - $99/mo target
One organization. ICM brain, public-source discovery, matching, writing, tracking, core team collaboration.

### Operator - $299/mo target
Multiplayer team workflows, Composio integrations, proactive scans/watchdog, browser-assisted portal work, more capacity.

### Private - from $999/mo target
Local/private deployment, QVAC model/RAG option, custom retention/policy controls, deployment support. Price is a hypothesis until deployment cost and support burden are measured.

### Studio Managed - $1,500-$3,000/mo hypothesis
Software + human review/strategy/exception handling for organizations that want outcomes without operating the system themselves. Do not promise award results.

### Future Nonprofit OS
Expand from grants into fundraising, reporting, donor operations, program administration, governance, and capital readiness after Grant Agent proves the shared factory.

## Launch sequence
1. Dogfood with New World Kids and record baseline labor, errors, abandoned grants, context gaps, and screen time.
2. Run the system on real NW Kids opportunities until the workflow is boring and repeatable.
3. Build one evidence-heavy internal case study.
4. Run 3-5 founding nonprofit pilots with observed workflows.
5. Publish measured before/after process evidence.
6. Sell through the Studio and direct nonprofit/fiscal-sponsor partnerships.
7. Add consultant/agency workspace only after single-org workflow is excellent.

## Distribution
- founder-led nonprofit/grant education content
- before/after workflow demonstrations
- "watch the agent do the boring part" computer-use videos
- content showing the human leaving the chair while work continues
- partner/referral channel with fiscal sponsors, nonprofit associations, grant consultants, accountants, capacity-building organizations, and eventually community-finance partners
- free funding-fit diagnostic as acquisition wedge
- targeted grant calendar / opportunity brief generated from public sources
- case studies organized around work removed, errors prevented, and opportunities surfaced rather than AI features

## Sales demo
Do not demo dashboards.

Give the agent a real nonprofit website + approved documents and ask:

**"What should we fund next, and what do you need from us?"**

Show it:
1. build the organization context;
2. find candidates with citations;
3. reject weak opportunities;
4. prepare one packet;
5. identify a missing fact;
6. ask the correct teammate;
7. continue after that answer;
8. stop at the human approval gate.

The strongest demo ends with the user doing less, not typing faster.

## Website direction
The marketing site must not look like generic SaaS. Follow the Collins-Level protocol: strategy before styling, proof before claims, content before containers, self-evident navigation, proprietary art direction, real case evidence, and independent review.

Recommended governing idea: **The Invisible Department.** Treat actual grant documents, evidence, team decisions, field notes, deadlines, and completed work as editorial material. Avoid floating dashboards, bento grids, fake metrics, AI-brain imagery, generic gradients, and feature-card walls.

## Proof required before pricing/market claims
Measure:
- inference/tool/infrastructure cost
- research recall on a known test set
- false-positive/false-negative fit rate
- draft factual error rate
- human review time
- portal task success
- scope/privacy failures
- support burden
- screen time removed
- work completed without human re-entry
- missed deadline/report rate
- application throughput
- award outcomes with attribution caveats

## Go-to-market kill criteria
Do not push harder on external sales if internal use cannot prove:
- reliable context retrieval
- source-cited matching
- safe team collaboration
- material reduction in repetitive work
- reviewable application quality
- durable state after restarts
- clear escalation behavior

If the system cannot create an advantage for New World Kids, the market offer is not ready.
