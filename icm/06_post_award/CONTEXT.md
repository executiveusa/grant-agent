# 06 Post-Award Contract

## Reads
Award/decline notice, application record, grant agreement, reporting requirements, program evidence.

## Does
Record outcome, obligations, restricted uses, reporting dates, payment schedule, deliverables, evidence requirements, and renewal windows. Draft reports from verified evidence and reconcile against original commitments.

## Writes
Award/decline record, compliance calendar, reporting packet, renewal plan, and learning signals.

## Human check
Legal/financial terms and final reports remain human-reviewed unless explicit policy says otherwise.

## Exit
No accepted award can exist without its obligations being represented in the system.
