# Prototype V1 — Post-Award Boundary

Post-award reporting, renewal, compliance calendars, and evidence collection are part of the Nonprofit Operating System roadmap but are not implemented in the first executable slice.

The V1 learning ledger should still retain enough provenance to connect a future award or rejection back to:
- original funding need
- opportunity source
- match decision
- human corrections
- final pursuit decision

This gives the later Reporting/Renewal worker a durable lineage without pretending the capability exists today.
