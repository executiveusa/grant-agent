# Design Direction

The product and marketing site must not look like generic SaaS. No floating dashboard hero, bento feature wall, gradient AI brain, or endless rounded cards.

## Governing idea
**The Invisible Department** - sophisticated grant operations are happening, but the software recedes until judgment is needed.

## Product behavior
Start with a quiet conversational field:

**What do you need funded?**

The agent responds in plain language, works in the background, and surfaces only three operational states:
- **Working** - no action required
- **Needs you** - a decision/fact/approval is blocking progress
- **Ready** - a meaningful artifact/result is complete

There can be deeper records and administration, but they are secondary and discoverable, not the front door.

## Marketing-site creative territories
### A. Invisible Department - restrained / recommended
Editorial, typographic, human. The page behaves like a confident institutional story rather than software marketing. Documentary nonprofit imagery collides with sparse grant-language fragments and evidence annotations. Signature moment: a funding need enters as one sentence, and the page reveals the invisible sequence of work behind it.

### B. The Funding File - expressive
The website borrows the visual logic of working grant files: tabs, annotations, stamps, citations, margins, proof marks, and revisions - elevated into art direction rather than fake bureaucracy. Strongest for demonstrating provenance and context.

### C. Mission / Machinery - experimental
Split emotional human mission material against an austere operational layer. As the user moves, the machinery progressively disappears and leaves the mission. High distinctiveness; highest usability/motion risk.

Owner selects the final territory before high-fidelity build. Do not average all three.

## Collins-Level bar
Use `wearecollins.com` as a quality bar for strategic confidence and craft, not as a style to copy. The internal protocol requires strategy before styling, self-evidence before experimentation, content before containers, proof before claims, independent council review, accessibility, responsive verification, and a 9.0+ primary conversion flow.

## Marketing story
1. Problem: every grant starts by rebuilding context.
2. Reframe: the work is not writing; it is a fragmented funding operation.
3. Product: a grants department that already knows.
4. Demonstration: give it one real funding need.
5. Proof: source-cited shortlist -> rejected weak fits -> prepared packet -> human gate.
6. Multiplayer: the whole team can contribute without re-briefing the agent.
7. Privacy: cloud or local/private architecture with clear boundaries.
8. Action: bring one real funding need / start a pilot.

Do not lead with model names, agent counts, ICM, Composio, QVAC, or computer use. Those are proof of architecture after the customer understands the outcome.
