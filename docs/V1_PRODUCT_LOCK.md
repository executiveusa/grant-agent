# Prototype V1 Product Lock

## Mode
Greenfield product prototype built on inspected upstream architecture and adapters.

## Outcome
Prove that one real New World Kids funding need can be converted into a smaller, source-cited set of worthwhile opportunities with less manual research and no invented facts.

## Target
Customer zero: New World Kids internal team.

Future target after proof: nonprofit teams with fragmented institutional context and insufficient grant-operations capacity.

## Primary action
Human describes what needs funding.

## Primary result
Grant Agent returns useful source-grounded work in one of three states:
- Working
- Needs you
- Ready

## Governing product philosophy
**Software that gets you out of your seat.**

AI should remove repetitive operational work rather than merely accelerate keyboard work.

## Protected architecture
- Canonical repo: `executiveusa/grant-agent`.
- ICM is canonical institutional context.
- Hermes is the Grant Director cognition/orchestration runtime.
- QM is the intended multiplayer identity/scope/policy/durable-state control plane.
- Deterministic/source-cited retrieval precedes model judgment where possible.
- Specialist workers are bounded subagents, not separate customer-facing products.
- API/structured automation precedes browser/visual computer use.
- New World Kids is proven internally before broad GTM claims.

## V1 constraints
- No external grant submission.
- No invented facts, metrics, eligibility, budgets, dates, status, or relationships.
- No credentials in Git/ICM/model prompts.
- No claim of hardened production authentication/multitenancy.
- No claim of fully local/private execution.
- No 9.5 claim without measured workflow proof and independent Gauntlet evidence.

## Proof required
- automated contract tests
- one real NW Kids funding-need run
- source URLs/provenance
- candidate rejection reasons
- missing-fact escalation
- measured human/screen time
- dependency versions recorded
- later: QM scope isolation, restart durability, critic pass, portal benchmark

## Owner approval
The owner explicitly approved moving forward with Prototype V1 and locked the phrase **Software that gets you out of your seat.** on 2026-08-13.
