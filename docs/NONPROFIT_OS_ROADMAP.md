# Nonprofit Operating System Roadmap

## North Star
Grant Agent is the first vertical of a broader nonprofit operating system. The system should reduce the amount of time nonprofit leaders spend sitting at a computer coordinating repetitive administrative work.

Brand philosophy: **Software that gets you out of your seat.**

The human sets goals, contributes judgment, approves consequential actions, builds relationships, and does mission work. The agent system handles repeatable research, coordination, preparation, monitoring, and execution underneath.

## Wedge 1 - Funding Operations
Grant discovery, matching, evidence, drafting, requirements, applications, portals, deadlines, reporting, renewal, and institutional funding memory.

## Wedge 2 - Fundraising Operations
Campaign planning, approved donor messaging, FundRazr/processor workflows, campaign traffic, supporter updates, partner outreach, evidence packaging, and reporting.

## Wedge 3 - Donor/Relationship Operations
Relationship history, follow-ups, stewardship, acknowledgements, segmentation, meeting preparation, and next-best-action recommendations under organization policy.

## Wedge 4 - Program/Impact Operations
Program records, evidence collection, outcome tracking, photo/document provenance, report preparation, partner updates, and reusable impact narratives.

## Wedge 5 - Governance/Compliance Operations
Board packet preparation, policy/document tracking, filing calendars, fiscal-sponsor workflows, audit readiness, and bounded compliance checklists. Human/legal review remains explicit where required.

## Wedge 6 - Capital Readiness
Build the institutional infrastructure required to become financeable:
- clean financial/document room
- grant/contract/receivable pipeline
- reserves and liquidity history
- banking relationships and documentation
- lender/investor requirement packets
- capital-stack research
- debt-service and covenant visibility
- organizational credit/readiness milestones

This lane prepares decisions; it does not itself authorize regulated lending.

## Future mission-finance path
Potential long-term paths to investigate with qualified legal/financial counsel include partnerships with mission lenders/CDFIs, SBA microloan intermediary models where eligible, and eventually a separate qualifying financing entity/CDFI structure.

Any lending capability must be treated as a separate governed business/charitable activity with jurisdiction-specific compliance, underwriting policy, servicing, disclosures, accounting, reserves, and risk management.

## Interface evolution
Stage 1: web chat + Working / Needs you / Ready.
Stage 2: Slack/email/mobile notifications.
Stage 3: voice-first interaction for common work.
Stage 4: ambient/wearable surfaces where the user can ask status, dictate facts, approve bounded actions, or receive escalations without opening a dashboard.

The state lives in the system, not in the interface. A future interface should be able to disappear without losing the work.

## Factory primitives
The reusable factory underneath all nonprofit operators should include:
- ICM organization templates
- QM identity/scope/policy/runtime layer
- Hermes orchestrator adapter
- specialist-agent contract library
- connector/authentication layer
- source/provenance registry
- computer-use execution layer
- approval/risk-policy engine
- audit trail
- evaluation/Gauntlet harness
- watchdog/learning loop
- local/private deployment mode
- export/rollback/sovereignty controls

## Expansion gate
Do not build every vertical at once. New World Kids must prove the funding workflow first. Expand only when the shared factory removes enough marginal build cost that a new vertical reuses most of the existing operating substrate.
